             removing RAG or multi-agent routing saves ≤0.53s while              the on-premise Typhoon-v2.5-30b matches GPT-4o at 3.3×
             RTF remains >1; architectural simplification would sacrifice        shorter TTFT and zero API cost. Second, TTS is the sole la-
